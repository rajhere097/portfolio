Projects

1. Databricks Delta Pipeline ETL

End-to-end ETL pipeline using AWS S3, Databricks, PySpark and Delta Lake. Reads raw Bronze Delta data from S3, transforms the data through Silver and Gold layers, applies structural transformations and data-quality processing, and exposes curated datasets for Power BI reporting.

2. Tableau Business Intelligence Projects

Business intelligence reporting projects using Tableau to analyze business performance, trends, KPIs and operational metrics through interactive dashboards and visual analytics.

7. E-Commerce Hypothesis Testing

8. Ride Cancellation & Revenue Loss Analysis

Analyzed ride cancellations by time, zone and vehicle category to identify operational patterns, driver-availability gaps and revenue-impacting cancellation trends.

9. Chocolate Sales Power BI Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing chocolate sales, revenue, products, regions, sales representatives and business performance using KPI-driven visualizations.

10. Diabetes Prediction
